# Code of Conduct

ZEHEN is built by people who got excited watching someone move holograms with their hands. Let's keep
the project as welcoming as that feeling.

## We expect

- Kindness and patience, especially with people new to code, Python or computer vision
- Feedback about the work, never about the person
- Credit where it's due, and owning our mistakes
- Respect for different hardware, languages, abilities and backgrounds

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language
- Personal attacks or deliberate intimidation
- Sharing anyone's private information without permission
- Spam, or repeatedly derailing discussions

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub
([@priyanshusta](https://github.com/priyanshusta)). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may
edit or remove content and block people who don't follow this code, in issues, pull requests and
any other project space.
