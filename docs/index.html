// Capabilities

Everything a mouse does. Then more.

01

Invisible camera

Tracking runs in the background. A see-through HUD draws a glowing cursor ring; your screen stays yours.

02

Air-writing

Pinch and write in the air. Windows' offline handwriting recognition types it into any app, with “did you mean” fixes.

03

Launcher

Hold an open palm: every open window, your favourite apps, search across all installed apps, system controls.

04

Two hands

Stretch both hands to zoom like a photo. Swipe your other hand to flick between apps.

05

Feels steady

One Euro smoothing, debounced gestures and click-lock make a hand as precise as a touchpad.

06

Stays out of the way

Touch your real mouse and ZEHEN lets go. Make a fist to pause. Guided tutorial on first run.

// Pipeline

From light to action in 33 ms.

See

The camera frame is mirrored and handed to MediaPipe, never stored or shown.

Track

21 landmarks per hand, both hands, every frame, fully on-device.

Understand

Finger poses, pinch distance scaled to hand size, debounced into a gesture.

Act

Real mouse, wheel and keystrokes, plus the HUD so you always see what happened.
